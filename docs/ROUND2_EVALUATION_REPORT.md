# Round 2 — AISHELL-1 Test Subset: Streaming ASR Endpoint Evaluation

**Experiment date:** 2026-10-11 (Pacific)

**Repository:** `E:\Code\voice`

**Status:** completed **30-utterance descriptive evaluation**; not a vehicle-cabin latency benchmark.

![Endpointing trade-off: corpus CER, early-endpoint proxy, and estimated on-time delay](../results/endpoint_tradeoff.png)

## Executive finding

On a fixed set of **30 AISHELL-1 test utterances from 11 speakers** (403 reference characters), increasing sherpa-onnx Rule2 trailing-silence threshold from **300 ms** to **1200 ms** reduced the observed **corpus CER from 64.76% to 13.65%**. The algorithmic **early-endpoint proxy** decreased from **27/30 (90%)** to **1/30 (3.3%)**, but the estimated mean delay among non-early endpoints increased from approximately **−1 ms (n=3)** to **977 ms (n=29)**. Because this delay is conditioned on differently sized subsets and based on an automatically estimated speech end, it cannot be interpreted as a validated user-facing latency improvement.

These are results for **read Mandarin audio, not car-control commands, physical microphone capture, TTS barge-in or double-talk**.

## Data custody / independent labels

- **Source and split:** AISHELL-1 **test**, 30 sampled from a test-only mirror (7,176 rows) with fixed seed `20261011`. The manifest has 11 distinct speaker IDs.
- **Original transcripts:** each accepted test ID's mirror text was cross-checked (normalized exact match) against `aishell_transcript_v0.8.txt` from the original AISHELL source. That transcript was not generated by the tested recognizer.
- **Original transcript SHA-256:** `b5f33b9e0b47548e20a5ea4e504297f80df41d559133924c4e5b7b544c15b5c4`.
- **WAVs:** 16 kHz, mono, PCM16; per-file SHA-256, source test ID and speaker ID in `../data/round2_endpoint_eval/manifest.jsonl`. Raw WAVs reside in a Git-ignored local folder; they are **not included in the commit**.
- **Selection receipt:** `../data/round2_endpoint_eval/provenance.json` preserves source identities, the selection seed and counts.

### Critical endpoint-label limitation

`speech_end_s` was inferred using **reverse RMS** over **25 ms frames / 10 ms hops**, with threshold **35 dB below the *peak frame RMS***; this is **not** an absolute −35 dBFS threshold and **not** a human timestamp. **28/30 WAVs** did not exhibit at least 50 ms of confidently detectable terminal silence under this method. Their estimated speech ends are boundary-sensitive. Do not call these values independently verified speech offsets or ground-truth speech-end latency.

Model-training contamination/overlap with these AISHELL samples has **not** been independently audited.

## Fixed experimental controls

One publicly released `sherpa-onnx-streaming-zipformer-zh-14M-2023-02-23` online Transducer, `sherpa-onnx==1.13.8`, greedy decoding, **160 ms input chunks**, **2 CPU threads**, and the same ordered manifest across all trials. The model's **native waveform-input feature pipeline** was used: the separate Round 1 **C++ Fbank was not connected**. Inference used **unpaced file replay** for stream-time measurement, not a hardware microphone or end-user wall-clock test.

Only the **Rule2 minimum trailing silence** threshold was changed: 300, 500, 700, 900 and 1200 ms.

## Measured results

| Rule2 threshold | S | D | I | CER (÷403) | Naive utterance-bootstrap 95% CER interval | Early-endpoint proxy | Mean non-early delay (eligible n) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 300 ms | 17 | 244 | 0 | **64.76%** | 54.20–75.34% | **27/30 (90.0%)** | −1 ms (3) |
| 500 ms | 27 | 101 | 1 | **32.01%** | 20.41–46.31% | **11/30 (36.7%)** | 352 ms (19) |
| 700 ms | 35 | 66 | 2 | **25.56%** | 15.09–37.91% | **7/30 (23.3%)** | 604 ms (23) |
| 900 ms | 43 | 36 | 2 | **20.10%** | 12.80–29.01% | **5/30 (16.7%)** | 665 ms (25) |
| 1200 ms | 46 | 7 | 2 | **13.65%** | 8.86–20.05% | **1/30 (3.3%)** | 977 ms (29) |

**CER:** corpus `(substitutions + deletions + insertions) / 403`. **Early-endpoint proxy:** endpoint stream time plus 30 ms is strictly earlier than the **automatically** estimated `speech_end_s`. All 30 trials yielded an endpoint at every threshold; the proxy measures only when the endpoint occurred relative to this imperfect acoustic reference. The **mean non-early delay** excludes early-endpoint events, so its eligible population changes (3 to 29 samples). Do **not** compare those means as a paired population latency improvement.

Observed deletions fell from **244** at 300 ms to **7** at 1200 ms; this is the major source of the CER difference. It is not direct proof that an acoustic echo canceller suppressed near-end speech.

### Independent post-hoc endpoint stream-time comparison

A later [paired endpoint timing audit](ROUND3_VALIDATION_REPORT.md) avoids the estimated speech-end reference entirely, computing **within-utterance finalization stream-time deltas** from the same committed result JSON. For 700→1200 ms thresholds, mean additional stream-time was **1179.2 ms**, median **320 ms** (30 matched WAVs), not a fixed 500 ms. These are algorithmic stream-time differences during unpaced replay, **not** verified device response delays. A production operating point remains unselected.

### Paired uncertainty estimate

A **paired speaker-cluster bootstrap** (11 observed speakers, 5,000 fixed-seed resamples) for **1200 ms minus 300 ms** yielded a CER difference of **−51.12 percentage points** and approximate **95% interval [−59.10, −42.55] percentage points**. See `../results/paired_endpoint_ci.json` and `../scripts/paired_endpoint_ci.py`. The per-threshold intervals in the table use **utterance-level** resampling and thus **do not** account for speaker grouping; the paired difference uses speaker-level grouping. Neither interval establishes in-car generalizability or independent training-run variance.

## Acceptance and limitations

1. **Source text**: independently matched, but no manually labeled speech start or end timestamps.
2. **Early cutoff and delay**: estimated against the reverse-RMS proxy. With 28 unclear tails, this is only an exploratory boundary indicator, not a validated post-speech latency measurement.
3. **Domain**: ordinary Mandarin readings, not car commands. No noise, AEC, TTS, vehicle microphone, online CMVN or Fbank-to-ASR integration evaluated here.
4. **Statistics**: 30 utterances and 11 speakers; the paired bootstrap supports within-set descriptive uncertainty, not a population causal claim or model train/test decontamination guarantee.
5. **Next independent validation**: manually annotate speech-end timestamps, add a genuine in-car command set and paced capture, then rerun paired thresholds. Do not choose a production endpoint threshold from this pilot alone.

External sources: [OpenSLR AISHELL-1](https://www.openslr.org/33/), [test-only mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TwinkStart/AISHELL-1), [sherpa-onnx online transducer models](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/pretrained_models/online-transducer/zipformer-transducer-models.html).
